What usually gets in the way

The website platform

On seven of your eight sites, this isn't a problem. They run on DealerOn, and they already serve both AI pages today. Bayway Buys Cars runs on a separate setup, so we'd confirm what's possible there.

It looks like one more line item

Most dealers first see this as additional spend. We'd look at it differently. Your eight home pages already score 83 to 100 on Google's SEO check, and SEO checks and fixes come with the Back Cover. Every month we run Google's tests, fix what we can with user access to your site, and send your website company the exact fix for the rest.

So before adding a line, look at what you pay for SEO today and what it gets you. If a vendor writes content and builds links, that's real work, and you may want to keep it. If you mostly get a monthly report, that's what we include.

What handing this over looks like

  • All your sites, one owner. You do this by hand today. We'd take it over across the group, and your websites keep the look they have now.
  • Vehicle facts, every day. A list built from your own inventory feed, with each vehicle's price, VIN and color, and miles on used cars.
  • The gaps closed. We'd correct the two llms.txt pages that fail Google's check and work out the missing one at Bayway Buys Cars.
  • Numbers you can check. A monthly scorecard from Google's own tests, and a dated log of every update.

We can't promise what ChatGPT or Gemini will say. We control what gets published, that it matches your feed, and that it stays current.

Step one is getting this right. You don't need ARD or WebMCP yet. Google's Chrome team has published how-to pages for both, and we'll tell you plainly when they start to matter for dealers.

What we'd need to know

  1. Your inventory feed. Is it one feed for all your sites, or a different one for each? Does it come from vAuto, DealersLink or somewhere else, and who's the contact?
  2. Your DealerOn sign-in. Is user access one login across the group, or one per site? Who can set it up?
  3. Bayway Buys Cars. Who hosts it, and who can make changes to it?
  4. Your current vendors. Who handles SEO for each site today, and when do those agreements renew?
  5. The decision. Who else weighs in on this, and who signs?
