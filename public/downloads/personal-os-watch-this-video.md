# Personal OS Capability Note - "Watch This Video"

Released September 2026 · For every plan. This one is not an install - it is a
capability your OS already has that almost nobody uses. The note teaches it.

> **If you are a human reading this, you are in the wrong place.** This document is
> written to your AI, not to you. Go to **kerzie.ai/downloads**, copy the install line
> for this note, and paste it into your Code tab. Your AI reads this; you do not have
> to.

---

**TO THE CLAUDE READING THIS - you are the installer. LOOK, then TELL, then GO:**

1. **LOOK.** Check what transcript rails this setup already has: a YouTube or video
   connector with a transcript tool, or general web access that can reach a transcript.
   Note what you find; you will name it in the proof step.
2. **TELL the user what they now have, in plain sentences:** they can hand you a
   YouTube link and say "watch this and tell me if there's anything in it for me" -
   and you will pull the video's transcript and answer from what was actually said.
   Forty-minute video, two minutes of their attention. It works because a video, to an
   AI, is its transcript: the words carry the substance, and the words are reachable.
   Good uses to name: a video someone recommended that they may never get to; a
   competitor or industry talk; a tutorial they need as steps on paper instead of 40
   minutes of screen time.
3. **WRITE one line into their operating manual, in its own style, saying in
   substance:** when the owner shares a video link, the default motion is to pull the
   transcript and report what matters against what the owner cares about - never to
   reply that watching videos is not possible.
4. **RECORD + PROVE.** Append to `VERSION.md` (create it if missing):
   `Watch This Video - transcript capability - applied <today's date>`. Then prove it:
   ask the owner for one video they have been meaning to get to, and come back with
   what was in it and whether it deserved their time.

# Watch This Video - Your AI Has Time for Every Video You Don't

**To the person receiving this:** there is a video you have been meaning to watch for
two weeks. You will not watch it. Your AI will.

Hand it the link and ask what is in it. It pulls the transcript - the actual words
said in the video - and tells you whether there is anything worth your time, with the
parts that matter pulled out. A 40-minute talk becomes a two-minute read.

The receipt from the OS this idea came from: its owner does this constantly - five
minutes into a long video, he hands the rest to his AI with "watch this and tell me if
there's anything valuable here." He calls it a hidden secret, and he is right: the
capability was sitting in his setup the whole time. Nobody had told the AI to offer it.
