Since September we've listened to the Swing Bays team: the vision session, the written wish list, time in the Parker store, the walkthrough on October 5, and the follow-up calls since. Every item below came from those conversations. This is the October 6 list, updated with what we know now: 84 things we'll build, and 36 things we'll need from your team to build them. Each box gets checked as it lands, so you always know exactly what you're getting and where it stands.

What we heard

  • Calls nobody can pick up go to a voicemail box that rarely gets checked.
  • Instagram and Facebook messages don't get a follow-up.
  • Club repair runs on a paper card at the counter.
  • Square is the only view of the numbers, and it's light.
  • Lesson videos get recorded but don't reach every channel.
  • No single record of who came in, from where, and whether they came back.
  • New stores should open with the same playbook, without starting over.
  • Everything already runs in the cloud, which makes connecting it simpler.
Nothing gets swapped out. Everything gets connected. Square, SimHouse, your website and your marketing all stay with the people who run them today. We connect them to one command center and feed them, so the same playbook runs at every store, with less of it done by hand.
