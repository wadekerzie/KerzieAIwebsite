Two things the return depends on

  • Supply. At 2% capture, 2,000 homes produce about 3,000 jobs in year 1, roughly 250 a month. At about 60 jobs a month per full-time contractor, that is 4 to 5 full-time equivalents across the trades in that footprint. The contractor engine has to be flooding at the same time as the homeowner engine, or the behavior flips to a busy signal.
  • The flip holding. The whole model rests on a flipped home not shopping again. Today's evidence is 3 of 27 back inside eleven weeks with nobody asking. The 90-day flood is the test of that number at scale, and the first thing to measure at day 90 is the repeat rate, not the job count.

What moves GotaGuy between bands is one number: the repeat rate. Prove the flip holds and the multiple climbs; the take rate and the job size are already receipts.
