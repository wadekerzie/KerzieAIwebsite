What we build for each store

  1. The identity page (llms.txt). Name, address, phone, hours, Ford, your service area, that your team speaks Spanish, and the notes an assistant needs so it never confuses Corpus Christi with Kingsville. Written once, revised when a fact changes.
  2. The inventory page (llms-full.txt). Every vehicle with year, model, trim, price, stock number, VIN, color, and the link to its own page on your site. Rebuilt every morning from your inventory feed.
  3. The daily check and receipt. We confirm both pages are live and readable, and log it every morning: cars in, cars out, price changes, total on the page. Nothing for you to log into.

The standard. We follow Chrome for Developers' llms.txt guidelines and check every store against Chrome's Lighthouse audit before and after placement. You get the audit receipt with the handoff. Your website platform stays. Your robots.txt stays the gatekeeper.

The SEO money: an offset, not new spend

The technical side of SEO, the part Google's tool scores, does not need a monthly retainer to stay at 100. It needs to be checked and the fixes handed to whoever runs your site. So that comes with the Back Cover:

  • A monthly scorecard for every store we cover: SEO, performance, accessibility, best practices, and AI readiness, from the same Google tool, with the trend month to month.
  • The fix list, in plain language, written so you can forward it as-is to your website provider: the items that keep SEO at 100 and the ones that move speed and AI readiness.
  • The AI pages no SEO package builds, rebuilt every morning.

What Google's score does not cover, and we do not claim to replace: your Google Business Profile, reviews, local rankings, and content. If someone does that work well for you today, keep them.
