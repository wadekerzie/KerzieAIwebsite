You already started this - and you did it right

Danny told us you'd built something on your own. You had. rmeurosport.com/llms.txt is live, 1,384 bytes, and was still being touched early this morning - somebody there is actually maintaining it. Two real gaps, both fixable without touching your website platform:

  1. The page points AI assistants at nine inventory endpoints on your own site - and every one of them returns a 403 Forbidden. The block is Cloudflare, on the DealerInspire platform underneath your site, not a setting you chose. Your own robots.txt does not disallow those paths, so the page is telling AI tools to go somewhere they're being turned away from at the door.
  2. The page carries no address, no phone, no hours, and no brands. An AI assistant asked who you are and where you sit gets nothing back from the file that's supposed to answer exactly that.

Honest caveat, in fairness to your own team: a 403 to an outside probe isn't proof that OpenAI's or Google's verified crawler is blocked the same way - Cloudflare validates real crawlers by network address, not by the name in the request. Worth checking from your side before assuming the worst. What we can tell you is what we observed today.
