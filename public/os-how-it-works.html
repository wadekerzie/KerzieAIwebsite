The 17 things your OS is built to do

A reference list. Some switch on in week one, the rest as you're ready.

  1. SetupIt's built from an interview about how you actually work: business owner, solo professional, or someone running their own role inside a company. You don't configure it. It builds itself around your answers.
  2. Voice and meeting captureVoice memos and meeting transcripts land in a folder it watches. It pulls the action items, files them, and starts the work. It stops at sending, publishing, spending, or contacting anyone.
  3. MemoryEvery session opens by reading one short briefing. Older detail moves to deeper folders. Nothing is deleted. You never re-explain your work.
  4. Stays leanThe briefing has a size ceiling. A weekly checkup flags anything that grew and moves it down a tier.
  5. Token efficiencyRoutine work runs on smaller, cheaper models. Judgment, your voice, and anything with money go to the top model.
  6. SkillsThe first time something works, the steps get written down, and it runs the same way from then on.
  7. ConnectorsEmail, calendar, documents, meeting transcripts and the tools you already use, each added with your yes. It works in your real systems, not a chat window.
  8. SecretsKeys and passwords live in one private folder that never leaves your computer. It can use them. It never shows them and never ships them.
  9. Routines with receiptsJobs run on a schedule. Each one writes a receipt: worked, nothing to do, needs you, or failed. A run with no receipt gets flagged.
  10. GuardrailsIt drafts and stages. Emails, posts and payments wait for your yes.
  11. Receipts biasIt checks the real thing before telling you something is true. Public content carries only claims it can prove. When it isn't sure, it says so.
  12. One place, every deviceThe folder can sync to private storage you control, so every device reads the same memory. Or it stays on one computer. Your choice.
  13. PortableThe memory and the rules are plain files in a folder, so another AI can run the same OS with a few file names changed.
  14. SpeedOn the newest models, the everyday work runs even faster.
  15. The paneOne screen: your moves, its moves, what's scheduled, and your rhythm.
  16. The night shiftOptional. Each night it reconciles the day's work into the record and lines up tomorrow.
  17. Dated work goes on the calendarIf you say yes, anything with a date that needs you becomes a calendar event with the full instructions inside.

Every OS is built for one person, from their own interview. Your connectors are the ones you and your company approve.
