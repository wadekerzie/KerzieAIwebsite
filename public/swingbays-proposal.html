Phase 2

Club repair, digital, and your posts on schedule

Club repair. The paper card goes away. A customer picks the grip, says how many, and types any special instructions, either on your website before they come in or on an iPad at the counter with the clubs under their arm. The order lands in the customer record. Colter gets one queue, in the windows he sets, so he can batch the work instead of stopping for every walk-in. When the clubs are done, the customer gets a text that says so. Repair work that's been driving twenty minutes to a big-box store has a reason to stay at your counter.

Your posts on schedule. Today somebody at Swing Bays remembers to post, or doesn't. Instead, you drop the content (the photo, the clip, the words) into a shared folder. We connect the Instagram, Facebook and Google profiles for every open store to one planner and post it by theme and by location, on a calendar you approve once. We don't write your content; you know your stores and your members better than we do. We make sure it goes out, every store, on time.
