What we do every morning, for every store

  1. Inventory in. Your feed lands, one combined feed split on your location ID or one feed per rooftop. We check it against the schema and compare it to yesterday.
  2. Pages out, per store. Two pages on each store site. The identity page: name, address, phone, hours, brands, service area, and the rules an AI engine needs to keep Chevrolet North and Chevrolet South straight. The inventory page: every vehicle with year, make, model, trim, price, mileage, VIN, stock number, and the direct link to its own page on your site, rebuilt daily. Boulder gets the pair on both the GMC site and the Cadillac site from one rooftop's feed.
  3. Read check. We confirm each store's pages are readable by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity and by a normal browser, and we stamp the time.
  4. Your receipt. One line per store per day: vehicles added, vehicles sold or removed, price changes, total on the page, time placed. One tab per rooftop. Nothing to log into.

Dealer.com stays. NativeRank stays. No scraping, no one on your side touches anything after the user add and the feed setup. We stay in our lane: these two pages on each site, maintained every day.
